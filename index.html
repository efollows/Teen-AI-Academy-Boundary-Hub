The Interest-Driven AI Sprint Track

Built around hyper-fixations, fast feedback loops, and flexibility. AI is used as a creative creative partner, NOT a lecturer.

1

Activity: World-Building Game Co-Creator

Take a favorite game or anime lore and ask AI: "Design 3 custom character abilities for my world, but make 1 totally useless."

Skill built: Iterative prompt refining and exercising decision-making over AI output.
2

Activity: Spot the Fake Fact

Ask AI to list 5 fun facts about an interest, but secretly instruct it to make 1 fact totally false. Can you spot it in under 3 minutes?

Skill built: Critical evaluation & healthy skepticism of AI answers.

The High-Focus Skill Mastery Track

Focuses on high-leverage workflows: academic synthesis, exam prep, debugging, and building personal productivity agents.

1

Workflow: Harsh Socratic Oral Examiner

Prompt AI: "Act as a strict exam board marker for senior biology. Ask me 1 practice question at a time and critique my answer ruthlessly."

Skill built: Active retrieval practice and rapid gap analysis.
2

Workflow: The Code & Logic Debugger

Never ask AI to write whole programs. Instead, paste raw error logs and ask: "Explain line 14's bug in plain English."

Skill built: Real-world software development & troubleshooting.
