The Human Sandwich Model

How to use AI without losing your ownership, critical thinking, or authentic voice.

🍞

TOP BREAD: YOU (Human Origin)

You set the goal, pick the topic, and decide what problem you want to solve.

🥬

THE FILLING: THE AI TOOL

Expands ideas, formats information, translates complex text, or spots mistakes.

🍞

BOTTOM BREAD: YOU (Human Ownership)

You double-check facts, edit the wording, and make sure the final result is truly yours.

RED: Stop (All You)

Core thinking builds brain muscle. Keep these tasks 100% human.

  • Writing initial draft essays or stories
  • Forming your personal opinions
  • Taking tests or assessments

YELLOW: Pause (Work as Partners)

Collaborate to explore, test, and refine ideas together.

  • Brainstorming project angles
  • Asking for practice test questions
  • Explaining a confusing concept step-by-step

GREEN: Go (Quick Assistance)

Delegate repetitive formatting, organizing, or checking tasks.

  • Formatting citations or tables
  • Checking grammar and punctuation
  • Turning messy notes into flashcards

Ethical AI Principles in the Real World

Four non-negotiable standards for using AI with integrity, honesty, and personal responsibility.

1

The Transparency Principle (No Stolen Credit)

Using AI to help you think or organize isn't wrong, but passing off AI generation as 100% human thought is dishonest.

Real-World Context (Job Interview / Application):

If you submit a project for a job or course application using AI text, and an interviewer asks you to walk through your choices on page 2, you won't be able to explain it if you didn't do the thinking yourself.

Golden Rule: Always be able to explain and defend every single choice using your own understanding.

2

The Verification Duty (Anti-Hallucination)

AI models do not search for truth; they predict language patterns. You are 100% responsible for any fact, stat, or claim you share.

Real-World Context (Buying Secondhand or Giving Advice):

If you rely on AI to tell you if a secondhand car or phone model supports a specific feature without double-checking the manufacturer specs or seller listing, you risk wasting real time and money when the AI turns out to be wrong.

Golden Rule: Treat every AI claim as an unverified rumor until confirmed on an official primary source.

3

Data Privacy & Security

Public AI models learn from what you feed them. Once information goes in, it leaves your control.

Real-World Context (Sharing Personal Info):

Pasting full personal documents, passwords, addresses, or private family records into ChatGPT stores that data on an external cloud server.

Golden Rule: If you wouldn't hand it to a stranger, don't paste it into a public AI prompt.

4

Respect & Fair Output (Anti-Harm & Integrity)

Using AI to generate dishonest content, copy someone's distinct creative work without credit, or create material that harms others crosses fundamental ethical boundaries.

Real-World Context (Event Promotion / Local Work):

Using AI to quickly edit background elements is practical, but fabricating fake reviews or copying a local artist's design word-for-word damages real-world trust.

Golden Rule: Use AI to amplify genuine capability—never to deceive, exploit, or gain unfair advantage.

Quick Sprint Mode

5-Minute Sprints

Best for fast exploration, high flexibility, or starting with a fun interest or hobby.

How to run a 5-minute sprint:

  1. Pick any interest or curiosity on your mind right now.
  2. Ask AI for 5 quick ideas or facts about it.
  3. Pick your favorite 1 and throw away the rest.
  4. Spot any claims that look inaccurate or hallucinated.

Deep Dive Mode

10-Minute Focus

Best for bigger projects, analyzing complex info, and mastering practical real-world workflows.

How to run a 10-minute deep dive:

  1. Paste long notes, articles, or project plans into AI.
  2. Ask AI to extract core points or compare options.
  3. Use AI as a critic to review your plan or application.
  4. Verify stats and claims against primary sources.
